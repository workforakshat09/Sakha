# Narration lines

Record each line as its own MP3 with the same voice and save it in sakha-site/audio/vo/ under the exact file name. Leave a short, natural pause at the start and end.

## Scene 02 · Meera

- `meera-01.mp3`: Five hundred years ago, in Chittor,
- `meera-02.mp3`: a young queen sang to Krishna every night.
- `meera-03.mp3`: The court began to whisper.
- `meera-04.mp3`: Her own family turned away.
- `meera-05.mp3`: They said she had lost her mind.
- `meera-06.mp3`: Meera heard every word.
- `meera-07.mp3`: Then she tied on her anklets,
- `meera-08.mp3`: and danced anyway.
- `meera-09.mp3`: Five hundred years later, her songs are still sung.
- `meera-10.mp3`: No one remembers the whispers.

## Scene 01 · The bow falls

- `arjuna-01.mp3`: Kurukshetra. Two armies. One man who could not move.
- `arjuna-02.mp3`: Arjuna let his bow fall.
- `arjuna-03.mp3`: His friend didn’t pull him up. He showed him how big the world was.
- `arjuna-04.mp3`: Bigger than this war. Bigger than this fear.
- `arjuna-05.mp3`: Then he told him one thing.
- `arjuna-06.mp3`: Do your part. Let go of the rest.
